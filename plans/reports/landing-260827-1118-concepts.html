The finding that shaped all three

Today's landing is a router, not a pitch.

It opens with “I'm a child / I'm a parent”, which only works for someone who already knows what Bubbli is. A landing page has to convert, and the converting audience is the parent: they decide, they consent (COPPA requires it), they pay. The child is the user, not the buyer.

Meanwhile the sharpest sentence you own is in PRD.md §1 and appears nowhere on the page: visibility into unsafe interactions — without exposing benign conversations. That clause is the entire answer to “why not just let them use ChatGPT?”, and every concept below leads with it.

Concept A

The Ladder

“Show them the mechanism. It is more reassuring than any promise.”

The severity ladder becomes the page. A visitor scrolls down through the tiers — ordinary chat stays private, a worry gets recorded, something serious reaches you — and understands the product without a feature list. Evolution of what you have, lowest risk.

Concept B

Two Truths

“One product, two experiences — held side by side the whole way down.”

The page splits permanently. Left is what your child lives; right is what you're shown. The argument lands through structure rather than copy, and the asymmetry is the promise: the left column is always fuller than the right.

Concept CRecommended

The Margin

“The page is a real conversation, annotated in the margin by the safety layer.”

A single scrolling transcript down the centre. In the margin, the safety layer narrates what it did at each turn — passed, recorded, opened, escalated. The product demonstrates itself. Nothing is claimed that isn't shown happening.

Start free — no card
  • 1Demonstration beats assertion. Every rival claims “safe AI for kids”. This shows the machinery deciding, turn by turn, which is very hard to copy credibly.
  • 2The middle turn is the brave one. Showing a real awkward question — the exact miss you caught in testing — signals you've thought about the messy case, not just the demo case.
  • 3The margin is where the trust is built. A parent reads the left column as their child and the right as themselves, in one pass.
  • 4“Three messages. One reached you.” gives a parent a number to hold, without inventing a statistic.
  • 5On mobile the margin note follows each turn inline, so the pairing survives a single column.
  • 6Reuses real components — bubbles, mark, severity chips, crisis card already exist. Cheapest of the three to build well.
Why I'd pick this one: it's the only concept that can't be written by a competitor who hasn't built the mechanism. A and B describe a promise; C shows a system making decisions, including an uncomfortable one. Risk: the crisis example is heavy for a landing page — it may need a softer default with the serious turn revealed on interaction.
